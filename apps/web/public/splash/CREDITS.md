# The canvas splash

- `atlas.webp` is the Atlas figure holding the globe, supplied by Ethan from his own file: black
  strokes on transparent, fading to nothing towards the feet. Its origin and licence are still
  to be confirmed (Q11 in `docs/reference/shell-polish/tasks.md`).
- `codex.svg` is an original construction drawn for this repo in the manner of a Leonardo
  notebook sheet: golden-ratio circles, the two diagonals and a measured horizon, all centred on
  the figure's globe. No licence is needed.
- Both are drawn as CSS masks filled with tokens, `--splash-ink` for the figure and
  `--splash-line` for the construction, so only the strokes show and they theme with the ink.
