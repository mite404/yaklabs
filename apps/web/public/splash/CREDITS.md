# The canvas splash

- `landscape.webp` (1280x853, about 254 KB) is Ethan's oil painting, an impressionist river
  valley, supplied by him for the splash. Its origin and licence are still to be confirmed
  (Q11 in `docs/reference/shell-polish/tasks.md`). It was resized from his 1536x1024 file and
  re-encoded at quality 76 with Pillow; nothing else about it was changed.
- It is the empty open space's ground under `--splash-hero-wash` (tokens.css), a moss wash deep
  enough that cream text clears 4.5:1 on a pure white pixel, so no crop of the painting can
  fail the words on it. The moss under the painting is the ground until the file arrives.
