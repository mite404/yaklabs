# The canvas splash

- `landscape.webp` (1280x853, quality 78, about 270 KB) is Ethan's oil painting of a river
  valley: green meadows and trees, blue hills, a cloudy sky. Supplied by Ethan from his own
  file; its origin and licence are still to be confirmed (Q11 in
  `docs/reference/shell-polish/tasks.md`). It was resized from 1536 wide with Pillow so it stays
  under 300 KB: the open space it fills is at most about 1000 CSS px wide.
- It is the ground of the empty canvas's open space, under a `--splash-wash` of the paper and
  the field's own dots, so it reads as lying beneath the dotted ground rather than on it. The
  copy and the button sit on a paper plate over it.
